01 Explore 2 Worlds

Grow the Nest.
Sustain the Colony.

Nest Expansion & Chambers

Assign workers to digging and the nest grows organically from existing tunnels. Food stores or brood chambers form automatically as needed.

Chambers raise food and brood capacity. When food storage is full, foragers wait and automatically resume as soon as space opens.

Adjusting the balance of roles to match the situation is the key to survival.

♛

Queen Power: High Risk, High Return

Raise Queen Power to lay eggs up to 3× faster. The tradeoff: the Queen gets hungry up to 8× faster, while nurses must feed her, carry eggs and tend larvae. Adjust power as the colony changes.
